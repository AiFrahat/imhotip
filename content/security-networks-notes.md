# Security and networks corpus review

Reviewed: 2026-09-21

- 100 original bilingual entries: 50 security, 50 networks.
- Each includes an Arabic name, definition, example, practical usage, three English fields, category, difficulty, related IDs, aliases, and primary references.
- Selection emphasizes broadly encountered concepts. It is an editorial selection, not a measured frequency ranking.
- Structural validation: 100 distinct slugs; no collisions with data.js or expansion.js; all related IDs resolve; all English triples are populated.
- Explanations and examples are original, concise educational wording. References were consulted through official pages or their retrieved search excerpts. Several direct NIST/CISA opens timed out; those references were checked through official indexed excerpts or replaced with accessible primary sources.
- Sources define concepts; the illustrative examples are authored examples, not claims of actual incidents.
- Older RFCs and NIST publications support foundational terminology. Entries do not recommend deploying obsolete protocol versions or security controls.
- TTL is explicitly scoped to IP packet lifetime. Roaming is scoped to Wi-Fi roaming. Socket is a network socket.
- No runtime, styling, publication, or original corpus files were modified.
